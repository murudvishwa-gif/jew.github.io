Quick wisdom

Three notes to remember

01

Store separately

Soft individual pouches prevent chains from tangling and polished surfaces from scratching.

02

Last on, first off

Apply perfume and cosmetics before wearing jewellery, then remove pieces before changing.

03

Inspect yearly

A regular professional check can catch a worn claw or clasp before a stone is lost.
