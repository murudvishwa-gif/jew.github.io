Good to know

Service questions

How long does a bespoke piece take?

Most designs take four to eight weeks after approval, depending on complexity and stone sourcing.

Can you repair jewellery purchased elsewhere?

Yes. Our atelier first assesses its condition and confirms what can be safely completed.

How is old gold valued?

We test purity in your presence and explain the day’s applicable rate before you decide.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but an appointment lets us prepare the right specialist and selection.
