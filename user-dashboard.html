Keep every piece radiant

Jewellery Care

Simple habits help protect the finish, settings and longevity of your jewellery.

Wear with care

Put jewellery on after perfume and cosmetics. Remove it before exercise, bathing or swimming.

Clean gently

Wipe pieces with a soft, dry cloth after wear. Avoid abrasives and household chemicals.

Store separately

Keep each piece in its own soft pouch, away from humidity, heat and direct sunlight.
