Our story

Gold, kept honest.

A family jeweller built on careful craft, transparent pricing and promises that last longer than a lifetime.

Since 1958

A small bench became a trusted name

Suvarna began with one goldsmith, one ledger and a belief that customers should understand exactly what they buy. Three generations later, that same clarity guides every design, certificate and conversation.

We honour traditional Indian techniques while creating pieces that feel at home in modern lives.

Our journey

Three generations, one promise

1958 · The first bench

A 200-square-foot workshop opens with a goldsmith and a handwritten ledger.

1986 · A family atelier

The second generation brings stone setting and bridal design in-house.

2010 · Certified clarity

Digital certificates and fully itemised pricing become standard.

Today · A wider family

Clients across India discover Suvarna online and in our Bengaluru flagship.
